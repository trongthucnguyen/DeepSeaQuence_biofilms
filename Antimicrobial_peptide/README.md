# Arctic deep-sea hydrothermal microbiomes as a natural niche for novel antimicrobial peptides

This repository contains the **code and data** used in the study:

> **Arctic deep-sea hydrothermal microbiomes as a natural niche for novel antimicrobial peptides**  
> Submitted to *Microbiome* (Springer Nature), December 2025

The study investigates antimicrobial peptide (AMP) discovery from Arctic deep-sea hydrothermal vent biofilms using integrated **metagenomic and metatranscriptomic analyses**, with a particular focus on long-read Nanopore sequencing data.
